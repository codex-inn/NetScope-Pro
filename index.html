Learning Mode

How to interpret observed signals and choose a safe next step.

HTTPS & certificate details

A successful HTTPS response indicates a TLS connection completed. It does not establish trust. The Worker cannot expose certificate expiry or chain fields; use your browser’s certificate viewer for manual review.

Security headers

Missing CSP, HSTS, frame protection, or nosniff headers are configuration signals. Review the site’s needs and deploy changes in a test environment before production.

DNS & email

SPF lists permitted email senders; DMARC tells receivers how to handle mail that fails alignment. DNS answers vary by resolver and time. Missing records are not proof of compromise.

Open TCP ports

An open port only shows that a TCP handshake succeeded from the Worker’s network. It does not prove a vulnerability. Closed or filtered is inconclusive.

Safe practice lab

Use a host you own or a dedicated lab target, add it to the Worker’s exact allowlist, confirm written scope, then select only approved ports. Never test third-party systems.

What this page cannot do

A hosted browser cannot inspect Wi-Fi, capture packets, reliably traceroute, enumerate LAN devices, or expose raw TLS certificate chains. No results are simulated.
